\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The process model of an organisation certifies which of its parts are independent. Using
that certificate for credit, for decision timing and for observation turns RL for
concurrent business processes from a monolithic problem into one that scales with the
number of processes: decisions are credited with the rewards they can influence, a
process can wait for the right resource without freezing the others, and each decision
reasons only about its own process. On an insurer benchmark, this lets the agent keep
its performance as concurrency grows and learn to wait exactly where waiting pays.
