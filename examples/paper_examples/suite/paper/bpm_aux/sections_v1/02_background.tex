\section{Background}\label{sec:background}

\subsection{Action-Evolution Petri Nets}
An \aepn~\cite{aepn2023} is a timed, coloured Petri net whose transitions are split
into \emph{actions}, fired by a decision maker, and \emph{events}, fired by the
environment (arrivals, completions, outcomes). Places hold tokens with attributes and
timestamps; a token can be consumed only once its timestamp has been reached. A
\emph{binding} of an action is an assignment of available tokens to its input arcs; at
every decision epoch the decision maker chooses one enabled binding. GymPN~\cite{gympn2025}
exposes an \aepn{} as an RL environment: the observation is a graph whose nodes are
tokens and transition bindings and whose edges follow the arcs of the
net~\cite{aepn_repr2025}, and the action space is the set of enabled bindings.
Fig.~\ref{fig:insurer} shows the \aepn{} of the insurer used in our experiments.

\paragraph{Postponement.} GymPN optionally adds a \emph{postpone} pseudo-action: instead
of firing a binding, the decision maker lets time advance to the next event. This makes
non-work-conserving policies expressible, at the price of freezing every decision in
the net until the next event.

\subsection{Policy optimisation on an \aepn}
Decisions happen at irregular times, so the decision process is a semi-Markov
decision process (SMDP). Let $k=0,1,\dots$ index decision epochs at clock times
$\tau_k$, let $r_k$ be the reward of all events firing in $[\tau_k,\tau_{k+1})$, and
let $\Gamma_k = e^{-\beta(\tau_{k+1}-\tau_k)}$ be the continuation discount. With a
critic $V$, GAE in SMDP form computes
\begin{equation}
  \delta_k = r_k + \Gamma_k V(s_{k+1}) - V(s_k), \qquad
  \hat A_k = \sum_{l\ge 0} \Big(\lambda^l \prod_{j<l} \Gamma_{k+j}\Big)\, \delta_{k+l},
\end{equation}
and PPO~\cite{ppo2017} maximises the clipped surrogate objective with these advantages.
Every decision is credited with $r_k$, the reward of the \emph{whole} net.
